How it works

The assistant is one script tag. It loads a 3D alien head (three.js), listens for things you do on the page, and speaks with an ElevenLabs voice (your own key, saved only in your browser) or your browser's free built-in voice.

Open its settings with the ⚙ button next to the alien. You can mute it, make it quieter, choose what it reacts to, switch characters (squeaky, Klingon-style warrior or robot), tune pitch and speed, and move it to the other corner.
